# User Manual — Front Desk Hotel App

A downloadable user manual (Word document, plus a PDF copy) explaining how to use the app from first setup to checkout. Nothing in the app, data or settings will be changed.

## What the manual covers

1. **Getting started**: opening the app, the one-time owner account setup, signing in, adding the app to the phone's home screen.
2. **Owner setup (Settings)**: hotel name, logo, address, GSTIN, default GST rate, check-in time, numbering prefixes, payment methods, receipt footer and policies. Extras (Continental Food, European Food, Extra Bed): setting prices, adding new extras, switching them off.
3. **Rooms**: creating categories, adding rooms (including ranges like 101–120), editing rooms, deleting rooms and when deletion is blocked.
4. **Staff**: adding receptionists, setting permissions, turning off access.
5. **Making a booking**: guest details and returning guests, stay dates and guests, picking one or more rooms by category, per-room rates, source, GST Bill on/off, discount, advance (future dates only), and same-day automatic check-in.
6. **Booking screen**: the room list, adding extras, guest IDs (photo/PDF upload and typed name + ID number, more than one guest), collecting payment, receipts and bills, and sharing on WhatsApp.
7. **Changing a booking**: removing or adding rooms, changing dates, cancelling with or without a charge, refunds or adjustments when the guest has overpaid, and the change history.
8. **Check-in and check-out**: when Check in appears (arrival day after the set check-in time), the balance rule and who can override it, the Check out step, and the final invoice.
9. **Daily use**: the Today screen, searching bookings, the Payments list, and Reports with CSV downloads.
10. **Customer documents**: Booking Receipt compared with GST Bill / Tax Invoice, and why customers see room categories instead of room numbers.
11. **Using several devices**: logging in on more than one device, and refreshing to see changes made elsewhere.
12. **Common questions and troubleshooting**: common error messages and what each one means.
13. **Quick reference**: a one-page daily checklist for receptionists.
